QUICK GUIDE

A useful recording starts here.

  1. Choose a source. Enable the microphone, select its input and type, or open an existing audio file. The test signal lets you explore without a microphone.
  2. Choose what matters. Speech focus is for human voices. Birds and weather use gentle filters and a background-only noise profile.
  3. Listen and compare. Use headphones for live monitoring. Watch the waveform and spectrum, adjust EQ, and solo a band to find a troublesome frequency.
  4. Record, then refine. Capture original and filtered audio together. Select a take, trim it, change settings and render again from the original.
  5. Save the result. Download audio and a JSON preset. A higher export rate does not add detail that the microphone never captured.

What cleanup can do. Reduce rumble, hum, steady noise and many disturbances around speech. Overlapping sounds can remain. Birds, hail and rain are not automatically separated into individual stems.

Recording limits. The initial version supports foreground sessions up to ten minutes and bounded file imports. Switching devices, backgrounding or locking a phone can interrupt capture. Partial takes are kept where storage permits.

What stays local. Audio, takes, analysis and presets stay in this browser. This page loads application assets; it does not upload your audio or filenames.
